# Safety and Interpretation / 安全与解释边界

AOAA is a research visualization tool, not a truth machine.

AOAA 是研究可视化工具，不是绝对真理机器。

## Boundaries / 边界

- Do not treat metaphor categories as final academic judgments.
- Do not rank people, teams, institutions, or papers purely by AOAA outputs.
- Do not upload confidential or private documents unless you understand the risks.
- Do not send sensitive documents to external AI APIs without permission.

## Interpretation / 解释原则

Atmospheric metaphors help users observe patterns. They do not replace peer review, domain expertise, or careful reading.

大气光学隐喻帮助用户观察模式，但不能替代同行评议、领域专业知识或严谨阅读。
